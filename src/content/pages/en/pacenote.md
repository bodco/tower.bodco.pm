## What's inside

<p class="pnx-sub">One app for the whole call: before, during and after.</p>

<div class="pnx-phases">
<div class="pnx-phase"><p class="pnx-phase__tag">Before</p><h3 class="pnx-phase__title">Walk in prepared</h3><ul class="pnx-list">
<li><i class="pnx-i pnx-i--brief" aria-hidden="true"></i><span><b>A brief from your files</b>Folders, Notion, Google Docs, earlier calls.</span></li>
<li><i class="pnx-i pnx-i--agenda" aria-hidden="true"></i><span><b>A live agenda</b>Ticked off as the call goes.</span></li>
<li><i class="pnx-i pnx-i--target" aria-hidden="true"></i><span><b>The primary document</b>Add the file the call is about.</span></li>
<li><i class="pnx-i pnx-i--calendar" aria-hidden="true"></i><span><b>The next call on Home</b>Every calendar, its readiness, one button.</span></li>
</ul><p class="pnx-phase__more"><a href="/pacenote/details/#before-the-call">More about the brief</a></p></div>
<div class="pnx-phase"><p class="pnx-phase__tag">During</p><h3 class="pnx-phase__title">The fact, right when it matters</h3><ul class="pnx-list">

<li><i class="pnx-i pnx-i--reply" aria-hidden="true"></i><span><b>A ready answer</b>When someone states what your prep answers.</span></li>
<li><i class="pnx-i pnx-i--help" aria-hidden="true"></i><span><b>HELP NOW</b>Ask mid-call, with your brief and files.</span></li>

</ul><p class="pnx-phase__more"><a href="/pacenote/details/#when-it-speaks">When it speaks</a></p></div>
<div class="pnx-phase"><p class="pnx-phase__tag">After</p><h3 class="pnx-phase__title">Notes already written</h3><ul class="pnx-list">
<li><i class="pnx-i pnx-i--notes" aria-hidden="true"></i><span><b>Notes and a follow-up</b>Decisions, what is on you, risks, a draft.</span></li>

## Private by design

<p class="pnx-sub">Speech to text, search and voices run on your Mac. Only text goes to the AI you choose.</p>

<div class="pnx-band">
<div><p class="card__num">0 bytes</p><p>of audio leave your Mac</p></div>
<div><p class="card__num">1-2 s</p><p>to a fact card from your prep</p></div>
<div><p class="card__num">$0</p><p>the app; about $13 a month for 10 calls on a key</p></div>
<div><p class="card__num">Any</p><p>call app: Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack</p></div>
</div>

<p class="smallprint">Google access is read-only, except the Doc you export; tokens stay in the macOS Keychain. Google is reviewing the app, so sign-in shows "Google hasn't verified this app" for now (Advanced, then Go to Pacenote). <a href="/pacenote/privacy/#google-user-data">Google user data</a> · <a href="/pacenote/pricing/">Cost table</a></p>
