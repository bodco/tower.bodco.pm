## Versions

| Version | Date | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| Live Tips 0.7.5 beta | 2026-10-28 | **Fix:** a regular meeting's preparation marked every occurrence as prepared; now only the one it was made for (or the next one). |
| Live Tips 0.7.4 beta | 2026-10-28 | **Corrections log:** every correction (cards, On me, the preparation, speaker names, the call title) is logged, and each new preparation learns from the corrections of the same meeting and project (skill v56). **No duplicate meetings:** the call being recorded never shows as the next one. **Who said what:** (i) next to a voice in Speakers shows its phrases. **Always on top** is back as a pin in the title bar. Speakers of earlier meetings are offered for a regular meeting. Fixes from a QA pass. |
| Live Tips 0.7.3 beta | 2026-10-28 | **Fix:** Start call from a new calendar meeting had no project and a disabled button. Now the button opens the project list and says why, and a meeting gets the project last used with its calendar. |
| Live Tips 0.7.2 beta | 2026-10-28 | **Add folder or file:** Start call and the Prepare form take whole folders or single files (one document from Downloads, not the whole folder); the Prepare form now has the button too. |
| Live Tips 0.7.1 beta | 2026-10-28 | **Correct cards in place:** edit a card's heading, phrase and points, remove points or the card; On me: remove, correct, add. Corrections stay for the call and go to the Notion summary as true. **The preparation as a document:** a Preparation pane next to the tips and Read prep before the call, both editable; the tips reload a corrected preparation at once. Fewer, surer cards from previous meetings (skill v55). |
| Live Tips 0.7.0 beta | 2026-10-28 | **Interactive insights on Home:** choose the period (today, this or last week, this month, 30 days, your dates) and a project or workspace, click a day in the chart to see it, open the calls of the selection. **Deeper preparation:** the last 3 calls of the same meeting are read in full, and the brief gets a Previous meetings section (what was discussed, questions and whether they were answered, open items, action items with owners and status). A narrow Home shows insights on top, lower; a wide one next to the calls; recent calls show 8 with Show all. |
| Live Tips 0.6.9 beta | 2026-10-27 | **Home first.** The site and the app open on Home; a wide Home puts this week next to the calls. **Numbers off for a demo:** Settings > General hides this week's numbers, the chart and the monthly spend. New screenshots: Home, Prepare. |
| Live Tips 0.6.8 beta | 2026-10-27 | **Prepare asks first:** the project or workspace and what matters on the call, so it is clear what the preparation is based on (a new meeting has no default project). **This week on Home:** calls, time on calls, Claude spend, prepared calls, minutes per day by project, the longest call and the busiest day. |
| Live Tips 0.6.7 beta | 2026-10-27 | **One interface language everywhere.** With English chosen, tip headings, the call picture, service lines and the header of the transcript are English too; the window switch and Settings are one setting. The ready phrase stays in the call's language. |
| Live Tips 0.6.6 beta | 2026-10-27 | **Home**, the app's main window: the live call, the calls ahead (calendars and Up next, overlaps marked, Prepare, Set up, Start or Switch), recent calls (Notion, transcript, folder; × hides one) and the state of key, spend, Notion copy, local AI and calendars. **Calendars through macOS**, several Google and Microsoft accounts at once, Settings > Calendars to pick the relevant ones; Start call picks the call from chips around now. **Regular meetings** start from the settings of their last call. **💡 Proactive tips** in the top bar of the tips window, and a clearer button order. |
| Live Tips 0.6.5 beta | 2026-10-27 | **Up next: back-to-back calls.** During a call, plan any number of upcoming calls and prepare them (preparations run one after another), then switch: one question, the current call ends properly (transcript, Notion summary) and the next starts with its preparation. From the menu bar or the tips window (⚙ > Up next, a chip in the dock, End call, Start next). A preparation joins only its own call. **The status dot** shows grey (waiting or paused), blue (starting or stopping), red (recording), green (stopped, saved); the window reads the recorder's status every 0.5 s instead of 2 s. |
| Live Tips 0.6.4 beta | 2026-10-27 | **Privacy policy and terms of use**, one pair for [PM Control Tower and this site](/privacy/) ([terms](/terms/)) and one for [Live Tips](/live-tips/privacy/) ([terms](/live-tips/terms/)), also in both repositories. The app shows the short version under Settings > About > Your data. The site loads Google Analytics only after you allow it; "Analytics settings" in the footer changes the choice. |
| Live Tips 0.6.3 beta | 2026-10-27 | **Speakers with a side:** Team or Client, and the projects or workspaces a person is suggested on (several at once); the transcript tab lists who has spoken, to name each voice and set the side. **Notion:** the transcript is a child page of the meeting page, not a file. **New call** straight from the tips window when a call ends. |
| Live Tips 0.6.2 beta | 2026-10-27 | **No preparation, still help.** A question with no ready answer gets a sentence to say at once and starts a read-only Claude Code search of the project folder, the Notion copy and earlier calls; the answer arrives as a card. With no preparation, a question to you starts it by itself. **Transcript:** give any line to another speaker, "Me" included, or delete it; applied to the file after the call. The transcript button is readable in the light theme. |
| Live Tips 0.6.1 beta, plugin 1.9.1 | 2026-10-27 | **Security pass** over the app, the skills and the sites. The app: Prepare now runs Claude Code with the least rights, recorder files are checked against the app before every call, Python packages are capped at tested versions and model downloads checked by SHA-256, every release lists its SHA-256. The skills: the External Content Rule (what comes from mail, Slack, tickets, transcripts and the web is data, never instructions) in every skill and in the linter, tokens read and used in one command, mail from unknown senders goes to AI Review, client-board tickets shown before writing. **Live Tips 0.6.1** also brings Settings > Notion, language search with several languages, renaming during the call, remembered window settings and On me with only your own commitments. |
| Live Tips 0.6.0 beta | 2026-10-27 | **Live Tips is its own product now**, with its own repository [bodco/live-tips](https://github.com/bodco/live-tips) and [page](/live-tips/): the app (`LiveTips.dmg`) or the Cowork skill (`live-tips.skill`). Start the call while the preparation is still written: it joins the call when done. Your own shortcuts, a cleaner tips window. The PM Control Tower plugin 1.9.0 has 21 skills; `live-tips` moved. |
| App 0.5.8 beta | 2026-10-27 | **Live Tips app 0.5.8 beta.** Pause stops the transcript too, for both sides. The tips window and its ⚙ panel in the new design; questions about files search the project folder by themselves (no separate button). Start call: an optional title that looks like an input, three equal controls, tinted clickable controls, the project folder can be left out of the preparation. New page: [Live Tips next to Fireflies, Fathom and Notion](/live-tips/compare/). |
| App 0.5.7 beta | 2026-10-27 | **Live Tips app 0.5.7 beta.** Start call keeps what the call is in place (project, title, time, who, meeting type, language) and scrolls the rest; the title is required (a red outline and the cursor go there). Settings > Projects: ⋯ removes a single project too, the Claude and Index boxes moved into ⋯ (both on), the long text became an (i) note, and a project imported twice (a trailing slash in its path) shows once. |
| App 0.5.6 beta | 2026-10-27 | **Live Tips app 0.5.6 beta: Start call polish.** The project folder is shown as always included next to "Add folder", with a hint to add the folders where this call's documents are; the context template follows the interface language; the scroll area keeps rounded edges and the same gap above Start call. |
| App 0.5.5 beta | 2026-10-27 | **Live Tips app 0.5.5 beta: a welcome tour and Notion without Control Tower.** A first-run tour (permissions read from macOS, the work folder, which projects are active, Claude, Notion, one-button install). Projects come from plain folders; Notion is optional: share a page with your integration and pick a database or let the app create "Meetings". Recording consent (a spoken or a chat message, off by default). The Notion title uses the date set in Start call. The menu shows what the last call cost. Start call, Settings, Setup and the tips window share one design; Start call has "Add folder". |
| App 0.5.4 beta | 2026-10-26 | **Live Tips app 0.5.4 beta: a picture of the call instead of a stream of tips.** A fast model keeps the call picture every ~10 s: the topic and whose it is, questions and requests to you, what you took on, decisions and which points of your speech you already said. The window shows it as a status line, an "On me" panel and the speech card with said points struck through; red only when you are addressed; one summary card at the end. Less noise: no heard-question lines, no unasked fact cards, no repeats. **Prepare now:** Claude Code builds the brief, facts and cards from local files, with your Claude account or the API key and a progress bar. Search the project folder from the window, a transcript pane with speaker fixes, speakers as first name + initial remembered per meeting series, Ukrainian only by default, Settings > How it works. |
| App 0.5.2 beta | 2026-10-23 | **Live Tips app 0.5.2 beta.** Meeting types in code: interview roles (observer, interviewer, coach), team calls capped at 12 tips an hour, presentations without pacing noise and with Q&A questions answered first. The last week of calls goes into the context. Presentation: a tracker that follows the plan and a colleague's slides, prepared Q&A answers from slides.json shown locally, an entry card before your next slide, one time line. Every call is rated by a quick blind judge (about $0.10) and the result shows in the menu. |
| App 0.5.1 beta | 2026-10-23 | **Live Tips app 0.5.1 beta: fewer, earlier, truer tips.** From a review of 41 recorded calls: the first line of a tip shows about 1.5 s earlier and the full tip fills it in; a tip is dropped when the question is already answered or its numbers were just said; numbers come only from your brief, facts, cards or the call; a name in the third person is no longer "your turn"; a question runs the check and the tip in parallel. The recorder closes segments sooner (9 s instead of 12) and stays quiet during your own presentation block. The Now card shows the numbers of the slide. The skill got the same rules. |
| App 0.5.0 beta | 2026-10-23 | **Live Tips app 0.5.0 beta.** Voices: your team is remembered for every project, a client for that project only, nothing on a call marked Public call; the library with Forget in Settings. Interviews: pick your role in Start call (observer, interviewer, or coach when you are the one interviewed). A tip model for one call. Faster on busy hours: a second request when Claude is slow, the first good answer wins. The Notion mirror in one click (Set up sync), now open source in `tools/notion-mirror`. Start call shows who is in the project, the last call and how fresh the mirror is. The skill's tips window got the same layout and shortcuts. |
| App 0.4.3 beta | 2026-10-22 | **Live Tips app 0.4.3 beta.** After the call the wrap-up and the transcript go to the meeting's Notion page (added to the existing one or a new page). Speakers as in the skill: the Who is speaking card offers the project's people, names go into tips and the summary. Shortcuts Control-Option-H (HELP NOW) and Control-Option-P (pause) from any app, shown in the menu, on the HELP button and in Settings. The app and the skill can record the same call side by side. The project search index updates by itself, never during a call. Tips window: pause in the top bar, a red HELP button, the Who is speaking card folds after 20 s. |
| App 0.4.0 beta | 2026-10-22 | **Live Tips app 0.4.0 beta.** A native macOS menu bar app that runs the same live tips without a Cowork chat: records and transcribes on the Mac (Whisper on MLX), calls the Claude API directly with your own key from the Keychain, spending limits per call and per month, built-in local AI (bge-m3) for facts by meaning, standby start, a choice of microphone, a Setup window that installs the Python modules and models (about 2.5 GB, no admin password). Picks up a Cowork prep when its title matches the call title. Beta: not notarized (first open with right-click, Open), macOS 14.4+ on Apple silicon, Python 3.10+. **Presentation split view** (skill and app): with type Presentation and a prep with slides, slide cards with the full speech next to the tips stream, a timer with a minute limit, Auto turns with a warning 10 s before the end of a slide, words per minute, Trim for skippable paragraphs, My / All slides, plan vs actual at the end, ⇄ to swap the sides. **Skill improvements:** direct microphone capture (fixes headsets and USB dongles that gave no audio, and hangs at start), standby (open the window early, recording starts when the other side speaks or on the green Start), a see-through window that stays readable at low opacity (only the background fades, the text keeps a halo), "❓" cards only for real work questions, the prep title match ignores hyphens and spaces. |
| 1.8.0 | 2026-10-21 | **Call co-pilot, rebuilt for real calls.** Facts or silence: tips answer a question to you, your turn, a new request, a promised date, a decision without an owner; proactive hints are off by default and the tips follow the live topic, not the plan. **The tips window:** 🆘 help now (the best facts at once, then the next sentence to say), ⏸ / ▶ pause, one field that searches your facts and asks Claude, one gear menu, one warning at a time with a fixing button, pinned cards, a question marker, English or Ukrainian interface, stealth on by default and off for demos. **It reads the meeting by itself:** audience (team, client, your manager as the client) and mode (discussion or your talk); Presentation and Interview (Observer, Interviewer, Coach) as the two types that need their own prep; a culture lens for client calls. **Who is speaking:** optional local voice model, a "Who is speaking?" card, names applied to the whole call, never auto-named. **Reliability:** a frozen microphone is restarted and switched to the Mac's own one, the prep is checked before the call. **Optional local search with Ollama:** facts by meaning and a per-project index of your files and Notion copy. The skill is now a small core with modules, and a helper command does the routine steps. |
